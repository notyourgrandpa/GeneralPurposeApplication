# GeneralPurposeApplication

A directory of applications I develop. Each project has its own repository,
documentation, and setup instructions.

## Description

- These are simple applications I have developed, am developing, or plan to build for everyday life.
- The ideas come from my own needs and things that could help other people.
- These projects are also part of my study, knowledge acquisition, and familiarization with .NET, Angular, and TypeScript.

## Projects

### 1. [Kita](https://github.com/notyourgrandpa/Kita) — Variety Store Management System

**Status: Under development. Moved to its own repository.**

_My mother has a sari-sari store. If I make a sari-sari store management system, it might help her._

- Help sari-sari store owners track item stocks, daily revenues, sales, and expenses.
- Development, source code, documentation, and setup instructions are now maintained in [Kita](https://github.com/notyourgrandpa/Kita).

### 2. Motorcycle Maintenance Tracker

**Status: Planned.**

_I can't remember when I last changed my engine oil. I should make an application for this!!!_

- Help motorcycle owners track timely maintenance for their motorcycles.
- May include mileage tracking to work out when an engine oil change is due.

### 3. Password Manager & Vault

**Status: Planned.**

_I have so many accounts: my 10+ Gmail accounts, my old CrossFire account, LoL accounts... What's my password again?!!_

- Help people with many accounts keep track of their passwords securely.

### 4. Water me, Plantito

**Status: Planned.**

_I love watering my plants, I love taking care of my plants, I love my plants! I should create an app because I love plants!!!!!!!!!!_

- Track plant watering intervals.
- Keep notes and details for each plant.
- Include a plant dictionary with care information and warnings.

### 5. Budget tracker???

**Status: Planned.**

_I need an application to allocate my funds per day so I can budget my money more efficiently._

- Allocate funds per day.
- Show budget allocations in a calendar view.
- Use charts to compare budget allocations and actual spending.

### 6. What's the Food for today????

**Status: Planned.**

_I want to track my food stocks in my refrigerator; I need an app for this._

- List ingredients from available food stocks when planning meals.
- Automatically manage food stocks based on the ingredients added to a planned meal.
- Allow temporary ingredients for planned meals when those ingredients are not in stock.

### 7. Test your Japanese!

**Status: Under development.**

_I'm learning Japanese. There should be an app to test my Japanese writing, reading, and listening skills!_

- Practice recognizing random characters from Hiragana, Katakana, or Kanji.
- Learn Japanese characters.
